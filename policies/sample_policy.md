# Prior Authorization Criteria — Dupixent (dupilumab)

## Indicated Diagnoses
Dupixent is covered for members with confirmed moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis
(ICD-10: L20.0, L20.8, L20.9) who have had an inadequate response to at least
one topical corticosteroid of moderate or high potency.

## Step Therapy Requirements
Member must have documented trials of:
1. Topical corticosteroid (Class I–V) for a minimum of 8 weeks
2. Topical calcineurin inhibitor (tacrolimus or pimecrolimus) if corticosteroid contraindicated

## Quantity Limits
- Initial authorization: 2 syringes per 28 days (loading dose)
- Maintenance: 1 syringe per 14 days
- Renewal requires documented clinical improvement (EASI score reduction ≥50%)

## Auto-Approval Criteria
Claims meeting ALL of the following may be auto-adjudicated:
- Valid ICD-10 code from approved list (L20.0, L20.8, L20.9)
- Prescriber is board-certified dermatologist or allergist
- Quantity within approved limits
- No prior auth denial within the past 12 months

## Denial Criteria
Claims should be denied if:
- Diagnosis code is not on the approved indication list
- Step therapy requirements have not been documented
- Quantity requested exceeds formulary limits without clinical justification
